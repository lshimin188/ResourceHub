# Business Resources 商业资源

## Creator Metrics

### [Koveto-Vasarlas.hu Engagement Rate Calculator](https://koveto-vasarlas.hu/tools/engagement-rate)
- Description: Calculate likes plus comments as a percentage of followers, reach or views; inputs are processed in the browser, with no account or profile lookup.
- Category: Business / creator metrics
- Language: Hungarian interface
- Free/Paid: Free calculator; the parent website separately offers paid social-media services.

Saves and shares are excluded. Different denominators are not directly comparable, and the tool does not forecast sales or classify a profile as good or bad.
